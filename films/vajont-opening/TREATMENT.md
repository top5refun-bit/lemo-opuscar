# TREATMENT — *The Dam Didn't Fail. The Mountain Did.*

Vajont, Italy, 1959–1963. Documentary opening, 67.77 s (2033 frames at 30 fps), 1920×1080.
Style: **Visual Investigation** (`styles/visual-investigation/STYLE.md`), unchanged.
Narration: the user's ElevenLabs recording, used whole and untouched. On-screen text and subtitles: English.

## 0. Material

16 scene packages from the user's Drive (`source/scenes/`), 28 unique images: 24 archival photographs (1955–1963 and one modern colour photo), one photographed information-panel map (retrospective), and four AI reconstructions made by the user in Higgsfield (AI01 geologist, AI02 geology concept, AI03 rockslide, AI04 wave). The style reference video and the numerical simulation are **reference only** and never appear in the film; the simulation taught one thing: the mass moved north as one block across the gorge and pushed the water both ways — up-valley towards Erto/Casso and west over the dam into the Piave valley.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Strict clock** — the narration is already chronological (1959 → 1963 → today). The readout in the picture changes form at each section: a year (`1959`) → a span (`1959 → 1963`) → a date and time (`09·10·1963 22:39`) → a duration (`< 60 s`) → `TODAY`. Findings drop in where the narration names them.
2. **Cold open on the frozen aftermath** (the dam standing in the wreckage), then rewind to 1959. Strong, but the narration opens on "In the summer of 1959": a cold open would fight the voice for the first 3 s, and we may not re-cut the narration.
3. **Two columns that converge** — the father's structure (DAM) and the son's evidence (MOUNTAIN) run as two parallel evidence columns; each section adds a card to one column; at the end the columns meet at one place on the map and the verdict board reads DAM ✓ STANDING / MOUNTAIN ✗ FAILED.

**Chosen: 1 carrying 3.** The clock structure follows the voice exactly; the DAM / MOUNTAIN pairing is the through-line that pays off in the last scene (the poster frame). Opening image: the gorge before the dam, as hard evidence with its real date (1955) next to the story date (SUMMER 1959). Ending: the evidence map — every card links to the dam on the valley map — then the editorial verdict.

## 2. Logline and arc

A son finds that the mountain above his father's dam is an old landslide waiting to move; four years later it moves, and the dam — perfect, intact — stands at the centre of the disaster.

- **Setup** (0–15.3 s): place, son, dam, father. Two people, one valley.
- **Discovery** (15.3–28.9 s): the mountainside is not solid; it fell before; it can fall again.
- **Turn** (28.9–48.3 s): 9 October 1963. 260 million m³, under a minute, the wave, Longarone.
- **Cost** (48.3–51.6 s): almost 2,000 dead. Stillness.
- **Paradox** (51.6–67.77 s): the dam survived; precision; the verdict.
