# Unfair Doodle — changes from Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon (scifi-toon)

Revision v2: the base is now Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon (v1 was built on Stick-figure Explainer, which is no longer used).
Borrowed moves from: Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`), Sketchnote (`sketchnote`), Whiteboard Explainer (`whiteboard`).

| Section | Base | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kept | thick dark outlines constant in screen space, flat fills, hard-edged shadow shapes, big white reaction eyes, elastic mouths, brows carry the emotion, reaction close-ups | the same | the look of the reference: bold, flat, reactive |
| §2 Line & boil | ~7 px, boil 1.7 px, everything boils | 13 px bodies, host and props (9 px face details); boil 0.6 px; no boil on the host, text and badges | bold readable line at phone size; a calmer, cleaner surface |
| §2 Cast | invented sci-fi duo, creatures | filled cartoon people: cream #FFFDF6 bodies, bean torso, tube limbs, mitten hands, nub feet, head 1:3 kids / 1:4 adults, one flat clothing colour; dot eyes by default, big white eyes on reactions; extreme expressions that switch instantly | everyday family cast for comedic science |
| §2 Host | no mascot | the host: a suited ivory die (navy suit, mustard tie), big filled brows ≈ 1/4 of the face each, big oval eyes, mouth with teeth and tongue, same outline weight; the star, in 60–70 % of shots | a recurring presenter that carries the channel |
| §3 Colour | one palette per world, colour-band skies, a glowing portal | a flat cream void #F7F3EA, no ground line; solid full-screen black / coral / navy for emotional or data beats; coral only for the subject's T-shirt and the key word; mustard only for tie, badges, crown; green only for scan and ticks | one accent keeps its meaning; colour beats replace worlds |
| §4 Type | Baloo 2 subtitles with speaker pills, retro terminal tags | hand-lettered ALL CAPS: Permanent Marker, Patrick Hand (≥ 36 px), Luckiest Guy; pops, slams, labels written on the word; no burned subtitles; 5 % safe margin on every frame | the channel's comedic voice |
| §5 Motion | on twos (12 fps) at 24 fps | 30 fps, boil at 12 fps; pop-ins with strong back-ease, zoom punch 1.0 → 1.12 in 4 frames, 6–10 px slam shake | fast comedy timing, YouTube frame rate |
| §6 Camera | portal swirl wipes, two-shots | hard cuts every 2.5–3.5 s, a cold open of ~11 cuts each with a new ground or scale; full-frame host close-ups (face ≥ 80 %) on punchlines; characters at 40–60 % of the frame | retention pacing; never small figures in an empty frame |
| §7 Sound | synthesized theremin score, sci-fi foley, a two-hander of voices | ukulele, pizzicato and quirky percussion at ~110 BPM; cartoon SFX; music stops for deadpan beats; the user's narrator | energetic comedic score |
| §8 Native moves | parallel worlds, portal, duo contrast… | the host's roll, the rank title card, THE FINE PRINT, SIBLING SCAN with a sketchnote banner and green ticks, reaction close-ups, the slot machine, join the hands, the reveal with starburst and 4-point sparkles | recurring segments every video reuses |
| Not used | sci-fi worlds, portals, colour-band skies, creatures, terminal tags | — | not this channel's world |
